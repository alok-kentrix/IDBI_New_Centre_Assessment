How to read

  • Rank follows the estimated Total Business; the likely range shows how settled each estimate is.
  • Lead segments are the businesses rated High for that catchment — what to staff and lead with.
  • White space is branch density plus IDBI's own presence in the locality.
  • Format resolves digital readiness against walk-in demand.

Street-level choice

  • The assessment works on a point, not a town: a few hundred metres change the catchment, the competition and the estimate.
  • The Ajwa Road pair shows the effect on two points about a kilometre apart.
  • Up to three candidate points per locality can be scored and ranked the same way once their coordinates are shared.

What a centre report covers

  • A · Location profile — residents, households, income, age, class, lifestyle, education, occupation; what stands within 500 m.
  • B · Catchment & micro-market — income bands, household spend, consumer and financial markers.
  • C · Banking ecosystem & competition — every branch and ATM in the catchment, IDBI's presence, the pincode's deposit and credit market.
  • D · White space — branch density, IDBI presence, public–private balance.
  • E · Segment opportunity — thirteen segments rated High / Medium / Low, with the evidence.
  • F · Format suitability — digital readiness against walk-in demand.
  • G · Risk & caution — only the cautions that apply.
  • Business potential — the steady-state estimate for eleven measures; the factors behind the estimate with their link to business across IDBI's network and their effect on this centre.

Data behind every centre

  • Catchment — residents, households, income, age, SEC, lifestyle segments, education, occupation, spend and product affinities in the locality around the point (Kentrix locality summary, consumer database).
  • Surroundings — offices and employees, transit hubs, institutions, hospitals, retail and hospitality within 200 m and 500 m; footfall hot spots in the catchment.
  • Banking network — branches and ATMs of 23 named banks and all others inside the catchment.
  • Market — deposits by type and bank ownership (Q1 FY 2026-27); lending by product: disbursement, loans, ticket size, growth, lender mix, overdue levels (Apr 2024 – Jul 2026). Pincode level, Experian.

How the estimate is made

  • BranchPlannix learns from IDBI's existing branch network how catchment, surroundings, competition and market translate into business.
  • An ensemble of gradient-boosted and tree models is trained per measure; the best-performing combination is kept.
  • Steady state. Each centre is scored with the branch at age 1, 2, 3 and 4. IDBI's current network is almost entirely mature, so the model returns one figure for all four ages: the level a branch in this catchment holds once it has settled. That single steady-state figure is what this page shows; the first years' build-up is not estimated.
  • The likely range is the disagreement between the models of the ensemble.
  • Factors. For each centre the model's own attribution (SHAP) gives the effect of every input on that centre's estimate, read as a share of the estimate. Beside it the page shows how the same input relates to Total Business across IDBI's existing branches (rank correlation), so a reader can see both the general rule and what it does at this site.
  • Ratings place the centre's figure against the spread of IDBI's current network catchments: top third High, bottom third Low.

Scope notes

  • Candidate points — one point per centre assessed; three-locality street-level ranking follows once coordinates are shared.
  • Ramp-up — the build-up over the first years is not estimated here; a year-by-year path needs the monthly panel of comparable young branches (BranchPerformix).
  • Footfall — indices not yet captured for the proposed points; walk-in demand is read from hot spots, transit, offices and institutions.
  • Fraud incidence — not in the data of this assessment; overdue levels by product are shown instead.
  • NRI banking — not measurable from catchment data; international-travel affinity is an indirect indicator only.
  • Existing branches — the same report structure applies to existing branches (BranchPerformix) with actuals in place of estimates.
